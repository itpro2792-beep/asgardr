# Amsterdam Civic Notebook

**Live page: https://itpro2792-beep.github.io/asgardr/amsterdam/**

An unofficial family civic notebook for Amsterdam, New York — built from public
sources, with every claim labeled **confirmed**, **preliminary**, or **unknown**.

Not affiliated with the City of Amsterdam or any government agency. For official
guidance: [amsterdamny.gov](https://www.amsterdamny.gov).

## Current entries

- **The July 29, 2026 flood** — what happened, why Amsterdam floods two different
