# Source Dating

Standing rules for anything Asgardr publishes, civic or technical:

## The rules

1. **No non-public information, ever.** The public surfaces are built entirely
   from public sources and the fabric's own publishable artifacts. If a claim
   would need a private source, the claim does not ship
   (`public-surface-review` is the enforcement arm).
2. **Every claim sourced and dated.** The source is named and linked where it
   can be; the date is the source's date, not the day of writing. "Checked
   against `GSMA/leaderboard`, Aug 2026" is the house form — auditable and
   honest about when it was true.
3. **Verify before publishing.** Each claim gets a verification pass against its
   primary source. During the latest house pass, one claim failed verification —
   **it was removed rather than published.** Removal is the correct outcome of a
   failed check; softening the wording is not.
4. **Corrections stay visible.** Nothing is silently rewritten. A corrected
   claim shows the correction; a removed section leaves a note where it stood if
   readers may have relied on it.
5. **Unverifiable ≠ false.** A claim that cannot be verified but is worth
   carrying is labeled UNVERIFIED (see `claim-labeling`) — published with its
   uncertainty, or not at all.

## Practical pass

Before a page ships: list its factual claims; for each, confirm source, link,
and date are attached and the source still says what the page says. Anything
that fails gets removed, relabeled, or ledgered (`discrepancy-ledger`) — never
averaged. The pass itself is an artifact: note the date of the sweep on the
page or in the ledger, so staleness is measurable next time.
